Scene 10: The Conclusion — Fade to Black
-----------------------------------------

Animation: Begin on the 0's table — the table the video has been
building throughout. It continues growing in the directions
established:
  - Downward: exponentially, rows multiplying with each new place
  - Outward left: natural number indices extending, digits
    accumulating, prefixed by an ever-growing column of 0's —
    reflecting the [0,1) constraint of the 0's holey table
  - Outward right: real numbers extending, digits accumulating
  - Pi visible in the table, highlighted.

Narration:
  "Infinity isn't a value.
  It's a function.
  For any input i, return i + 1.

  And when you apply it carefully -
  systematically, not randomly -
  you find that the natural numbers and the real numbers
  grow together.
  Always have.

  Same size. Same cardinality.

  Aleph zero."

Animation: As the narration lands, the camera begins to rise.

Rather than the table growing upward to dissolve the [0,1)
constraint, new holey tables are laid down *below* — each
a complete Holey B.I.B.L.E. table for a successive integer
range, appended beneath the last:

  - 0's table: [0,1) — natural number prefix column: all 0's
  - 1's table: [1,2) — natural number prefix column: all 1's
  - 2's table: [2,3) — natural number prefix column: all 2's
  - 3's table: [3,4) — natural number prefix column: all 3's
    Pi highlighted here in a distinct, slightly warmer color —
    3.1415... lives in this table. Its natural number index
    carries the 3 prefix. It was always here.
  - 4's table, 5's, 6's... accumulating below as the camera
    continues to rise

Each table is visually distinct — a separate holey section,
its own prefix, its own constraint — and yet structurally
identical to all the others. The pattern is the point.

Animation: As the stack of holey tables grows and the camera
rises further, the tables begin to tip on their side, arranging
themselves around a central axis to form a cylinder. Each holey
table is a distinct edge of the cylinder — one table per integer,
one edge per hole.

The camera continues to rise and pull back. The cylinder has
more and more edges — one for every natural number — and as
their count approaches infinity, the edges become less distinct.
The polygon stops looking like a polygon. The edge facing the
camera smooths, degree by degree, into a circle.

Infinity, made visible by the vanishing of its own discreteness.

The circle fills the frame. Fade to black.

Note: The upward camera movement replaces the previous "table
growing upward" animation. The constraint dissolution is now
conveyed through the accumulation of holey tables and the
camera's rise above them — the audience escapes [0,1) not by
breaking the constraint, but by seeing that it was always just
one hole among infinitely many. The cylinder-to-circle
transition is the visual thesis: infinity is not a destination,
it is a shape you can only see from far enough away.
