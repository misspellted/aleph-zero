1: Reference Resolutions
-------------------------

1.1 - REF-1 and REF-2 Confirmed as Same Video

The original storyboard carried two separate reference entries:
  - REF-1: Veritasium, "Math's Fundamental Flaw"
  - REF-2: Veritasium, "There's a Hole at the Bottom of Math"
    (also known as: "Math Has a Fatal Flaw")

Confirmed via YouTube (surfaced via a Reddit r/3Blue1Brown post)
that both titles refer to the same video:
  https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HeQX2HjkcNo

References collapsed into a single [REF-1] entry. The former
REF-3 (NDT/JRE) and REF-4 (source confirmation question) are
renumbered as [REF-2] and retired respectively.

1.2 - Timestamps Now Fully Documented for REF-1

All key moments in the Veritasium video are now timestamped:
  - Treatment begins: 3:45
  - Example table: 5:24
  - "no duplicates" (R column): 5:02-5:05
    "The key is to make sure we get them all [the real numbers]
    with no duplicates..."
  - "unique" (N column as index): 5:26
    "...each integer acting like an index number, a unique
    identifier for each real number on the list"
  - d-prime function flaw (9 -> 8, orphaning 0): 5:50-5:52
    "...if the digit is a nine, just roll it back to an eight..."
  - Conclusion: 6:22-6:34

1.3 - The "Unique" Keyword — Nuance Captured

The word "unique" in REF-1 technically describes the N column
(each integer as a unique index/identifier), not the R column
directly. However, "no duplicates" (5:02-5:05) applies the same
concept to the R column explicitly. Both are faces of the same
requirement: the argument depends on uniqueness holding on both
sides simultaneously. This nuance is now documented in the Scene 5
note in storyboard.txt.

1.4 - d-prime Flaw is a Choice, Not an Oversight

The 9 -> 8 mapping in the original d-prime function is an explicit
decision stated in REF-1 at 5:50-5:52. The consequence — 0 being
orphaned from the output space — follows directly from that choice.
This is now noted in Scene 4 of storyboard.txt. The flaw is in
the decision, not the arithmetic. This subtly strengthens FLAG-2.

1.5 - REF-2 Timestamp Still Approximate

The NDT/JRE timestamp (~2:26:00) has not yet been verified by
re-watch. Remains approximate pending confirmation.
(see backlog.txt)

2: Structural Decisions
------------------------

2.1 - Backlog Extracted into Separate File

The BACKLOG section that previously lived at the bottom of
storyboard.txt has been extracted into a standalone backlog.txt
file. Inline [BACKLOG: ...] notes in the storyboard were replaced
with [see backlog.txt: ...] pointers to keep the storyboard clean.
Completed items are marked [x], open items marked [ ].

2.2 - Scene 2 and Scene 3 Swapped

Original order:
  Scene 2: The Linear Brain (NDT/algae example)
  Scene 3: The Recapitulation (Cantor's argument)

New order:
  Scene 2: The Recapitulation (Cantor's argument)
  Scene 3: The Linear Brain (NDT/algae example)

Rationale: Presenting Cantor's argument first, then landing the
NDT quote — "The linear brain overrides even the stated facts" —
makes the quote function as a mirror rather than a warning. The
audience has just nodded along to the argument; the quote reflects
that experience back at them. Stronger sequencing.

The Scene 3 narration was updated accordingly:
  Old: "And it's about to happen to all of us. Again."
  New: "And you just watched it happen.
        Let's go back and look more carefully."

All internal scene cross-references updated to reflect the swap.

2.3 - Fishing/Carp Angle Considered and Rejected

During review of REF-2 footage, the idea arose of incorporating
Joe Rogan's "fish/carp" tangent as a hook — playing on a "fishy"
sense that something is wrong with Cantor's argument. Rejected for
the following reasons:
  a) Tonal mismatch — the video's tone is investigative and
     precise; a jokey hook works against that.
  b) The NDT quote already does the priming work, and does it
     with more weight and credibility.
  c) Using Joe's reaction would increase footage dependency,
     whereas the NDT quote can stand alone in narration.

Note: Joe Rogan derailing a conversation about exponential growth
with carp is also documented in session-notes-01.txt (section 5.5)
as a cautionary example. The irony of nearly being derailed by it
again in session 02 is noted for the record.
